In Sector 150, Noida (Sports City), PIN 201310, about 1 km from the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway and around 5 km from the Yamuna Expressway entry. It falls under the Noida Authority, not Greater Noida.

The project is planned on approximately 14.83 acres with around 900 residences. The tower count and floor details will be confirmed at launch.

2, 3 and 4 BHK residences are planned. Final sizes and carpet areas will be confirmed in the RERA-registered plans. Contact our team for the latest configuration details.
