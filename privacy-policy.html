1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit an enquiry form, we collect:

  • Your name (optional)
  • Your phone number (required)
  • Your email address (optional)
  • The type of enquiry, based on the button you used (for example "3 BHK" or "Site Visit")

If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we also keep a record of that conversation.

Information collected automatically. When you submit a form, we also record the page address you submitted from and any campaign tags in the link you followed (such as utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign). These tell us which advertisement or website brought you here. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the time of your visit.

We do not ask for or collect sensitive information such as financial account details, passwords, health information or government ID numbers through this website.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and share project details, pricing, floor plans, offers and availability
  • Arrange site visits and follow up on your interest in the project
  • Send you updates about this project and related residential projects that may interest you
  • Understand which advertisements and channels bring enquiries, so we can improve our marketing
  • Keep records and meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including under RERA

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Consent to contact (including DND)

By submitting your details on this website, you authorise us, and the developer where relevant, to contact you by phone call, SMS, WhatsApp or email about your enquiry and related projects. This consent applies even if your number is registered under the National Do Not Call (DND/NDNC) registry.

You can withdraw this consent at any time by telling us that you no longer wish to be contacted (see Contact and grievances). We will stop contacting you within a reasonable time.

4. Who we share it with

We share your information only where needed to serve your enquiry:

  • Our CRM provider. Enquiries are stored in Sell.Do, a customer relationship management system that manages leads on our behalf.
  • The developer. We may share your details with Prestige Group or its authorised representatives to arrange site visits, bookings or project communication.
  • Service providers. Our website host and communication tools (such as telephony, SMS, email and WhatsApp services) process data on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, court order or a government or regulatory authority, or to protect our legal rights.

These parties may use your information only for the purposes described here.

5. Cookies and third-party services

This website uses Google Tag Manager to load analytics and advertising measurement tools, such as Google Analytics and conversion tracking for our Google and social media ads. These tools may set cookies and collect information such as the pages you visit, how you reached the website and whether you submitted an enquiry. We use this only to measure and improve our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

The website also uses your browser's temporary session storage to show your first name on the thank-you page; this is cleared when you close the tab.

The location section embeds a Google Map. Google may set its own cookies and collect usage data when the map loads, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, the UP RERA website and other external sites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.
